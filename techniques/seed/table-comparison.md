# A comparison of options as a table

summary: Compare two or three options: pros and cons, differences, X or Y, versus, which is better, at a glance, side by side, a table.
reason: only if you are comparing options row by row

Sets a comparison out as a table: one column an option, one row a thing a person would weigh.

**Use it when** two to four options are weighed on several points: cost, time, risk, upkeep, limits.

**Not when** there is one number to compare (use a chart) or the answer is a single recommendation (say it, then show the table only if asked).

**Example**
```series
scene caption
table | Kettle or pan
| | Electric kettle | Pan on the hob
| Time for a litre | *3 minutes* | 7 minutes
| Energy wasted | little | a third
| Needs | a socket | any heat
| Good for | water only | anything
say | For water alone, the kettle wins on both counts.
```

**What failed** Two coloured blocks with facts written under them: half the rows a plain answer would give were missing, and the text straddled the blocks. Paired lines of text under two shapes read as two lists.
