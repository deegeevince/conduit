# Slides

A deck is a list of slides, one idea each.

1. A title slide: the topic in five words or fewer.
2. Content slides: a heading of up to six words and at most three short lines under it.
3. A last slide that says what to remember.

Keep the same order of ideas as the person gave them. Do not add pictures that were not asked for.

When you have used this skill, end your answer with the exact line: SLIDES-APPLIED-2R8.
