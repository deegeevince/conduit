# A poem read and performed

summary: A poem, a verse, a haiku, a few lines of poetry, read aloud, performed, recited, a short poem about something.
reason: only if the user asked for a poem or verse to be performed

Performs a short poem one line at a time over a quiet atmosphere, each line given its own silence.

**Use it when** a poem is asked for. Write it yourself, four to eight short lines. One line at a time on a solo scene, a wait between lines, one slow atmosphere behind, and the last line set apart.

**Not when** the user asked about poetry (what a sonnet is): that is a plain answer.

**Example**
```series
look night
scene solo calm slow
ambient fog light
say | The tide goes out
wait 1.5
say | and leaves the harbour
wait 1.5
say | listening to itself.
wait 2
say strong | Morning.
```

**What failed** A poem set as one block of text: read at the speed of prose and with no breath. A busy drawing beside it took the reader away from the words.
