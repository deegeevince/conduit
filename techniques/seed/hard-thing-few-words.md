# A hard thing said in few words, with time

summary: Advice on something painful or frightening, a hard conversation, a loss, bad news, fear, grief, a mistake to admit, being laid off, what to say.
reason: only if the user is facing something hard and needs few words, not information

Says a hard thing in three or four lines with silence between them, then offers a way on.

**Use it when** the user is hurt, afraid or ashamed. The pause before the line that matters does the work.

**Not when** they asked for steps or facts: give those plainly.

**Example**
```series
scene stack lines=3 calm slow
say | Say it plainly, once.
wait 2
say strong | Then *let them answer*.
cam push slow
say aside | Most of the fear is the silence before.
wait
say choice | help me find the words
say choice | what if it goes badly
```
