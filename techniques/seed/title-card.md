# A title card or an opening title

summary: A title card, an opening title, a title sequence, the name of a documentary, film, podcast, book or show set as big type, a heading screen, credits.
reason: only if you are making a title card or an opening title for a named work

Sets the name of a work as big type on an empty stage, with one quiet line under it and nothing else.

**Use it when** the user names a thing to be titled. Choose a look that suits its subject, centre one title with a slow entrance, and let one small line say what kind of work it is.

**Not when** the user asked for a length in seconds (a stated length also applies) or for a whole film: this is one card.

**Example**
```series
look night
scene solo calm slow align=center
say ghost | COLD
say title enter=type | The Far Shore
wait 1.5
say aside | a short documentary
wait 2s
```

**What failed** A title with a drawing behind it competed with the words. Two lines of equal size read as a list, not a title.
