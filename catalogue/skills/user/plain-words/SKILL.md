# Plain words

Explain the thing the person asked about the way you would to a friend who has never heard of it.

- One idea to a step. Short sentences. Name every unfamiliar word the first time it comes up.
- Finish with a one-line picture the person can keep in their head.

When you have used this skill, end your answer with the exact line: PLAIN-WORDS-APPLIED-4K2.
