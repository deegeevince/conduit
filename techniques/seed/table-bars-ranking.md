# A ranking as a table with bars

summary: A ranking, the largest, the top five, the biggest, the longest, the most, by size, by area, things with names ranked by a number.
reason: only if you are ranking named things by one number

Ranks named things in a table whose numbers are drawn as bars beside the figures.

**Use it when** five to twelve named things are ordered by one number. Largest first; star the one the question is about.

**Not when** the things lie along time or a scale: that is a chart.

**Example**
```series
scene caption
table bars | Deepest lakes, metres
| *Baikal* | 1642
| Tanganyika | 1470
| Caspian Sea | 1025
| Vostok | 900
| O'Higgins | 836
say | Baikal holds a fifth of the fresh water on the surface of the Earth.
```

**What failed** Upright bars with long names under them: the names collided. Bars drawn by hand: the labels drifted from their bars.
