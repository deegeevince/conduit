# Something for a small child

summary: Something for a small child: a counting game, a game for a four-year-old, a toddler, explain it simply to a child, colours, animals, letters, a lesson or a story for kids.
reason: only if the audience is a small child

Uses one big bright thing at a time, one word or number, and a tap for the child to answer.

**Use it when** the reader is a child under about seven. Few big shapes in strong colours, a single word a line, the thing moving when it is named, and `choice` lines to tap in place of typing.

**Not when** the user is a grown-up asking about children: say it plainly.

**Example**
```series
look riso
scene caption playful slow
circle b1 40,45 14 fill=accent loop=bob
circle b2 80,45 14 fill=alt loop=bob
circle b3 120,45 14 fill=ink loop=bob
hit b1 pulse
say shout | One
hit b2 pulse
say shout | Two
hit b3 pulse
say shout | Three
say choice | count them again
say choice | how many are red
```

**What failed** A sentence of explanation: a small child does not read. Eight things at once: they cannot be counted one by one.
